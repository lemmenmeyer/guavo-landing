Industries we fund

Restaurants & Bars

Kitchen equipment fails on a Friday. A slow January still owes rent. Guavo funds restaurants and bars with financing that moves with your sales instead of against them.

Medical & Dental

Your practice is busy and your bank account still looks thin, because insurance pays 30 to 90 days after you do the work. Guavo funds the gap.

Auto & Repair

Parts get bought before the customer pays. Guavo funds repair shops so a big job never turns into a cash flow problem.

Professional Services

You earn most of the year in a few months and carry overhead through the rest. Guavo funds the shape of that business.

Retail & C-Stores

Retail is inventory first and revenue second. You buy in September to sell in December. Guavo funds the gap in between.

Contractors & Trades

You buy materials and pay the crew weeks before the customer pays you. Guavo funds contractors through that gap.

Salons, Spas & Gyms

Your equipment is your service. When it dates, clients notice and go elsewhere. Guavo funds the upgrade before that happens.

Not listed here?
